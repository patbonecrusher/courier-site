Courier

A native macOS mail client for Gmail that sorts, summarises and helps you clear your inbox, using an AI model that runs entirely on your own Mac.

Your mail stays on your Mac. Courier has no server. It talks directly to Gmail, stores what it downloads in a local database on your computer, and runs its AI model on-device. Nothing you read or write is sent to the developer or to any third party.

What it does

Unified inbox

Several Gmail accounts in one window, each with its own colour.

Automatic sorting

Every conversation is filed under Priority, Personal, Notifications or Newsletters, with a one-line summary and an urgency rating.

Keyboard triage

Archive, star, snooze and file with single keys, with undo.

On-device assistant

Ask questions about your inbox. It can suggest actions, and nothing happens until you confirm.

Learns your preferences

Correct a category and Courier remembers it for similar mail.

Labels in Gmail

Categories are written back as Gmail labels, so they appear on your other devices.

How it uses your Google account

When you add an account, Google asks you to grant Courier access to read, compose, send and organise your Gmail. Courier uses that access only to do what a mail client does on your behalf:

  • download the conversations in your inbox so it can show them to you;
  • apply the changes you make, such as archiving, starring, marking as read, moving to trash and snoozing;
  • create four labels under Courier/ and apply them to conversations it has sorted.

The full details are in the Privacy Policy. Use of Courier is covered by the Terms of Service.

Availability

Courier is a personal project built by its author for their own use. It is not distributed through the App Store and has not been submitted for Google's verification, so Google shows an "unverified app" notice when an account is connected.
